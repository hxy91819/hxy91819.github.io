agent-gdrive

agent-gdrive is a personal, non-commercial tool. It lets its owner read files from their own Google Drive (including files other people have shared with them) on a personal server, using rclone with read-only access.

It is not offered to the public. Only the owner authorizes and uses it.

What it accesses

Read-only Google Drive access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly), used to download or export documents and spreadsheets the owner asks to read. It never creates, modifies, or deletes Drive files.
